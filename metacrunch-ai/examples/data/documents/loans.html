Loans

Everyone with a library card can borrow books, journals and media. Bring your card to the loan desk or use one of the self-service machines in the entrance hall.

Loan periods

ItemLoan periodRenewals
Books4 weeks3 times
Journals1 weeknone
DVDs and games2 weeks1 time

Renewals

You can renew a loan online in your account, by phone or at the desk, unless another reader has reserved the item. Each renewal starts a new loan period from the day of the renewal.

Overdue items

  • We send a reminder by email one day after the loan period ends.
  • Overdue fees are 0.50 per item and day, at most 10.00 per item.
  • Your account is blocked while fees exceed 20.00.

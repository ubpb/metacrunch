# Reading room

The reading room on the second floor has 120 desks, each with a power outlet and a lamp. Quiet is kept at all
times; group work is possible in the group study rooms on the first floor.

## Reserving a desk

Desks can be reserved for up to four hours a day in the online booking system. A reservation lapses when the desk
is not taken within 30 minutes of its start.

## What you may bring

- Laptops, tablets and notes
- Water in closed bottles
- Bags only in the lockers next to the entrance

## Reference collection

Dictionaries, encyclopaedias and the latest issues of journals stand on open shelves in the reading room. They
cannot be borrowed, but can be scanned for free at the scanners next to the windows.

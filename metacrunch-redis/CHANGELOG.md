# Changelog

## 5.1.0 (unreleased)

The first version for metacrunch 5, for Redis 7 or later and Valkey, on redis-client. It replaces metacrunch-redis
1.1.0, which belongs to metacrunch 4.x.

It lives in the metacrunch repository, shares metacrunch's version and requires metacrunch in exactly that version.

### Added

- `Metacrunch::Redis::Connection`: a `redis://`, `rediss://` or `unix://` URL, user and password, and any option of
  `RedisClient.config`; a client of its own for every reader and writer, checked with `HELLO` when it opens, Redis 7
  or later or Valkey; `CONNECTION_PARAMS`, a group of parameters (`params { use ... }`; the password as a secret),
  `Metacrunch::Redis.connection_params(prefix)` for jobs with two servers, and `Connection.from_params` for jobs and
  commands.
- `Metacrunch::Redis::QueueWriter`, a sink: one entry per record at the tail of a list, one `RPUSH` per batch (and
  per `max_bytes`); waits while Redis is full (`maxmemory`) or the queue holds `max_length` entries, and logs when it
  starts and stops waiting, until a stop (SIGTERM to a job whose source can stop) makes it give up the batch; warns
  about a `maxmemory-policy` other than `noeviction`.
- `Metacrunch::Redis::QueueReader`, a source: takes entries from the head of a list, `fetch` per round trip, until
  the queue is empty, or with `wait:` for new entries without end or until the queue stayed empty for a while; yields
  `Metacrunch::FLUSH` while it waits, so batched sinks and workers write what they hold within `flush_after`
  seconds, also between the records of a group; retries a lost connection and a server that is still loading its
  data while it waits; ends on SIGTERM after the records under way, and puts entries it took but did not hand on back
  at the head of the queue, also while Redis is full.
- `Metacrunch::Redis::KeyWriter`, a sink: the record as the value of a key from a template (`book:%{isbn}`) or a
  callable, with an expiry, `SET` or `DEL` per record, pipelined per batch.
- `Metacrunch::Redis::KeyReader`, a source: the String keys that match a pattern, with their values, by `SCAN` and
  `MGET`.
- Entries and values as JSON (`format: :json`, the default) or Strings as they are (`format: :string`).

### Changes from metacrunch-redis 1.1

- `QueueSource` and `QueueDestination` are replaced by `QueueReader` and `QueueWriter`, which take a `Connection`
  instead of a Redis client.
- `blocking_mode: true` is `wait: true`; the reader also takes `wait: seconds`, writes while it waits and stops
  gracefully on SIGTERM.
- Records are JSON in the queue by default, generated by the writer and parsed by the reader.
- The writer batches with `batch:` and waits on `maxmemory` for `pause` seconds (1) instead of 10, and also on
  `max_length`. `save_on_close` (a `BGSAVE`) is gone.
- No dependency on redis-rb and ActiveSupport.
